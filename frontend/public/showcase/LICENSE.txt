Demo dish models for the marketing home page showcase.

Source: Kenney "Food Kit" (2.0) — https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), public domain —
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Processed with the app's own pipeline: external texture embedded, scaled
to a real-world footprint (burger 12 cm, pizza 30 cm, cake 22 cm, skewer
25 cm) with ModelScalingService, and converted to USDZ for iOS AR with
backend/tools/usdz/gltf_to_usdz.py.

In production, restaurants' own dishes are their Tripo-generated models.
